THE CAUSES OF FAILURE FOR THE MAJORITY OF DESIGN WEBSITES

To make a profitable site, you need to know several key things about your target audience and your strategy for getting sales: What is your website visitor looking for? Who is and is not your ideal customer? What is the potential long and short term worth of each customer?

The answers to these and many more questions will impact the setup, substance, and structure of your site. Time and again, decisions are made without first asking the right questions, resulting in sites that customers are unable to connect with. Below are a few examples why websites often fall short in achieving their sales goals.

⦿ Absence of Elements that Build Trust

You need to be mindful that your messaging is relevant to your website visitors, and that it stays integrated and consistent across platforms. If you run a print advertisement the messaging a visitor to your site sees should reinforce what they saw in print. Otherwise, they may quickly leave your site even if it's designed really well. We evaluate your current and target customers along with your sales strategy for your website to best understand your company's needs and to ensure your messaging is appropriate and effective for your site.

⦿ Shortcomings with Website Development

Too often the coders who create your website design and the developers who create how it works behind the scenes cut corners.
